I'm a dad too

So I know a career decision stops being a solo one.

Some days I wonder why getting two kids up, dressed, fed and into school on time isn't on my LinkedIn achievements.

Dads do a lot that never makes it onto a CV. Part of what we do is work out what you actually want from the bit that does.

I'm a Career Coach. I won't tell you to quit and I won't tell you to stay. You bring what's on your mind, and we look at what you're assuming, what's true about your circumstances and what you want to do about it.
