For parents and supporters

If someone you care about seems stuck, you’re probably not imagining it.

What may be going on

The block usually isn’t about motivation.

Telling them to “just get on with it” probably isn’t working. They’re probably listening.

Someone can be capable and still find it difficult to take their next step. There may be several reasons, including circumstances that a short questionnaire cannot capture. Start by listening to how they describe it.

The GAP Profile is a free self-reflection tool with no sign-up. It can give them a starting point for thinking about their situation. It is not a diagnosis or a scientifically validated assessment. Let them choose whether to take it and whether to share their results. If you would like to discuss whether coaching could help, you can email me.

Want to find out if coaching would help?

Email me and we can talk about whether coaching might suit them. If they’d like somewhere to begin on their own, the GAP Profile is free and takes about five minutes. Whether they take it, and whether they share the results, is up to them.
